About this data

Locations are collected from publicly available Hive account profile location fields. The system classifies and geocodes those declarations automatically.

A location shown here is not proof of residence, nationality or a user's current whereabouts.

Why join Hive Census?

Hive Census is a separate, voluntary project. Participants choose a locality and publish a signed declaration on the Hive blockchain. This makes Census declarations more explicit than automatically interpreted profile data.
