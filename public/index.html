CURRENT CENSUS DECLARATION
Remove your current Census location?

This will publish a new hive_census operation:

{"v":1,"action":"unset"}

Your account will disappear from the current Census after the indexer processes the operation. Previous declarations cannot be deleted from the Hive blockchain.

2. Choose your city or town

Start typing a locality. Search also supports alternative, local and historical place names. Census uses the public centre point of the selected locality — not your home address or device GPS.

Permanent public record

Hive is an immutable public blockchain. A published Census declaration cannot truly be deleted. A later unset can make it inactive in Census clients, but the historical operation remains public.

WHY ON-CHAIN?

The website is an interface.
The blockchain is the record.

Hive Census is designed as an open protocol. If this website disappears, another client can read compatible hive_census operations and reconstruct the Census without our database or permission.
