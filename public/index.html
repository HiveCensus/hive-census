Permanent public record

Hive is an immutable public blockchain. A published Census declaration cannot truly be deleted. A later unset can make it inactive in Census clients, but the historical operation remains public.

WHY ON-CHAIN?

The website is an interface.
The blockchain is the record.

Hive Census is designed as an open protocol. If this website disappears, another client can read compatible hive_census operations and reconstruct the Census without our database or permission.
